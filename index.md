Fabrivoy Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-10-23

Fabrivoy is a note-taking app for supplier visits. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone. Everything you enter — supplier names, booth numbers, contacts, products, notes, answers, and photos — is stored only on your device. Fabrivoy has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Camera and photos. The app asks for camera access to photograph signs, business cards and products, and for photo-library access to attach existing pictures. If you turn on "Save to iPhone Photos app" in Settings, new photos are also saved to your Photos library (add-only access). Text on sign and business-card photos is read on the device itself; the photos are never uploaded.

Exports. When you export, the app builds a Word or Excel file on your phone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (email, Files, a cloud drive) is your choice; Fabrivoy does not send it anywhere.

Deleting your data. Delete a supplier, or use Settings → Delete all suppliers. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

Children. Fabrivoy is a business tool and is not directed at children.
